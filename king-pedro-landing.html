Not just another card app

King Pedro is a trick-taking game rooted in Ukrainian-Canadian prairie tradition — the kind played over cooling borscht while someone's baba keeps score on the back of an envelope. This brings that table to your phone, embroidery and all.

  • Full, verified King Pedro rule set — bidding, trump, and trick play
  • Two teams of two, seated across from their partner
  • A cozy kitchen table setting, not a felt casino floor
  • Every hand remembered — your opponents notice how you play
